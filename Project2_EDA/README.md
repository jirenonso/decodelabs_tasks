Performed EDA across payment methods, products, referral sources, and revenue trends.
Found Online as the most-used payment method (21.5% of transactions), with Printer and Tablet as the most-ordered products. 
Instagram was the top revenue-generating referral source/channel (259 orders, $275K+), ahead of Email — however, 
Closer investigation showed Printer and Tablet's revenue was driven more heavily by the Email channel specifically, despite Instagram leading overall;
Monthly sales trend revealed that Year 2023 generated higher total revenue than 2024 ($552K vs. $480.2K)
despite June 2024 being the single highest-revenue month in the dataset ($68K).
A box-and-whisker plot of TotalPrice showed a right-skewed distribution, mean significantly farther than the median indicating possible outliers in the dataset.

Tools and skills: Excel, PivotTables, Exploratory data analyis, Data visualization, Conditional formatting, box-and-whisker/distribution charts)
